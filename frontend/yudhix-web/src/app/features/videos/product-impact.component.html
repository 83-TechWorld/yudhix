How is this calculated?

Reach weights each comment by how many people engaged with it: 1 + likes + 2 × replies. The impact score is the negative share of reach, scaled by audience size (about 10,000 reach is the maximum): 100 × (negative share)0.75 × size. High is 60 or more, medium 35 or more. Sentiment and topics come from the local AI model for the most-liked comments, and from keyword rules for the rest.
