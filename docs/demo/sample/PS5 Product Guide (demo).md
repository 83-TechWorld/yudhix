# Storage
The PS5 Pro ships with a 2TB SSD. Storage can be expanded with a compatible M.2 NVMe SSD of up to 8TB.

# Disc drive
The detachable disc drive retails at $79.99 in the US. After a one-time online pairing, discs play without an internet connection.

# Controller
The DualSense controller battery lasts between 3 and 6 hours depending on the game and haptics settings. It charges fully in about 3 hours over USB-C.

# Warranty
Every console comes with a one-year limited hardware warranty from the date of purchase, covering manufacturing defects.

# PlayStation Plus
If a PlayStation Plus subscription lapses, cloud saves are kept for six months so players can resume when they renew.
